FlatHub — four visual directions

The same two screens in each direction, light and dark, drawn at true phone size (390×844). The type scale is a notch below V2: hero 68 (was 80), figures 15–17, row titles 15–16, labels 12.5, captions 11.5. One accent per screen; every coloured state also carries a word, so it survives a colourblind reader and a black-and-white print. Contrast ratios below are computed, not eyeballed.

The sample data is this flat's. Six mates — Sahid, Ansh, Adnan, Anant, Ayan and Jatin — at ₹2,000 each a month, with Ansh Rastogi holding the kitty. No invented flatmates, no invented shops: Zepto and the local kirana are the two places this flat actually buys from.

The cost of the floating bar, stated plainly. Only the active tab carries a label, so four of the five things in the bar are glyphs with no words — six flatmates have to learn a wallet, a fridge, a checklist and a chart. Add is the exception: it sits outside both capsules as its own circle, because it opens the spend sheet and must never look selected. Each direction says below how it keeps its four glyphs apart at 24 pt.

Direction 1 · light-first

Paper

For the flatmate who wants it to feel like Apple shipped it — cool grey canvas, inset grouped lists, nothing shouting.

iOS grouped-list DNA. Cool grey canvas, white inset groups with 12px corners and hairline separators, sentence-case headers sitting outside the group, chevrons on anything that opens. Indigo is the only accent and it only ever marks what you can tap. Inter throughout, tabular figures.

Switch: 280 ms, cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1). Measured label widths at 14/700 Inter: Kitty 32, Kitchen 52, Duties 43, Spending 64 pt. The leaving pill loses its label width and the group capsule gains exactly that much, both on the one curve for the full 280 ms, so the 7 pt gap never changes. The outgoing label fades to 0 over the first 80 ms with a −6 pt x-shift; the incoming one fades in from 120→280 ms with a +6 pt x-shift, so the two never overlap. The 40 pt badge keeps its indigo fill and crossfades glyphs over 140 ms centred on 140 ms, scaling 0.90→1. The entering icon leaves the group and the leaving icon re-enters it on the same curve and duration, so no glyph jumps. Press: scale 0.96 over 100 ms, release over 180 ms. Reduced motion: widths snap, only label and glyph crossfade, 120 ms. Measured geometry: at the 362 pt bar width the three group icons are 48 pt tall and 51–62 pt wide; the narrowest case is Spending active at 51 pt, which clears the 44 pt floor.

Four icon-only destinations. SF-weight 1.8 pt strokes at 24 pt, and four silhouettes that share no outline: the wallet keeps its card slot and stud, the fridge its door split and two handles, the checklist two ticks against two rules, the chart an arrowhead. None of them is a plain rounded rectangle.

Direction 2 · light-first

Masala

For the flat that cooks together — warm cream, a serif voice on every heading, petrol blue as the single accent.

The warm one, and the only one with a serif in it. Instrument Serif carries the wordmark and every heading, Inter carries the figures. Quick add stops being a grid of boxes and becomes a row of tappable 46pt chips; each fridge lot is its own soft card with an 18px radius, so the list breathes. The accent is a petrol blue, chosen so it cannot be read as the green of money coming back or the red of a loss.

Direction 3 · dark-first

Graphite

For reading the kitty at 1am — cool near-black, no cards at all, the tightest rhythm of the four.

The dark-first one. The balance sits straight on the canvas with a spend bar under it instead of inside a card; quick add is three compact columns; the fridge is plain rows divided by hairlines. Underline tabs rather than pills. Periwinkle is the accent and it is the only colour that is not a state.

Direction 4 · light-first

Ledger

For the treasurer — stark black on white, ruled like an account book, monospaced figures that line up to the paisa.

The monochrome one. Ink itself is the accent, which leaves colour free to mean exactly one thing each: green is money coming back, amber is soon, red is a loss. Rules instead of cards, 6px corners, dotted leaders between a name and its amount, and JetBrains Mono on every figure so the columns line up.

Switch: 160 ms, cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1) — mechanical, no scale and no overshoot, because nothing else in this direction bounces. Measured label widths at 12/700 Inter with 0.08 em tracking are KITTY 42, KITCHEN 61, DUTIES 50, SPENDING 70 pt; pill and group trade that width over the full 160 ms. The outgoing label fades over the first 50 ms with no x-shift, the incoming from 80→160 ms, also with none — letters appear in place. The ink badge crossfades glyphs over 90 ms at the midpoint at a flat scale of 1. The 8 pt corners and the 1.5 pt rules never move. Press: background shifts to the raised tone over 80 ms, no transform. Reduced motion: an instant swap — at 160 ms there is almost nothing to remove. Measured geometry: at the 362 pt bar width the three group icons are 48 pt tall and 47–56 pt wide; the narrowest case is Spending active at 47 pt, which clears the 44 pt floor.

Four icon-only destinations. 2 pt strokes at 24 pt and no fills anywhere, so silhouette alone carries it: the chart keeps its arrowhead, the checklist two ticks rather than one, the wallet its stud, the fridge its handles. In a direction with no colour to lean on, a vague glyph has nothing to fall back on.
