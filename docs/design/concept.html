V3 — the concept screens, drawn to be argued with

Eleven screens at true phone size (390×844). This page is about what is on each screen and where it sits, not about colour. Every screen carries a note saying which complaint it answers, in the owner's own words, so the wrong ones are easy to spot and say no to.

The colours are a placeholder. Nobody has chosen a palette yet. These warm off-whites are taken from ref-4 only so that the screens can be drawn at all — if you hate them, that is not a comment on this page. The things to judge here are: what appears on a screen, in what order, and what you have to tap to reach it.

The dashed line on a tall screen is the fold — 844pt, where an iPhone 14/15 stops. Anything under it needs a scroll. The floating bar is drawn at the bottom of each drawing so it does not sit on top of the content you are meant to read; on a real phone it stays pinned at the fold and content passes behind it.

Light only. Dark is not drawn, because a placeholder palette does not have a dark twin worth arguing about. Once a palette is picked, both modes get drawn.

The house rules still hold anyway: one accent per screen, every coloured state also carries a word, "Pay"/"Get back" and never "owe", no per-mate leaderboard, Indian digit grouping, tabular figures. The contrast table below is computed from the hex values, not claimed.

The data is this flat's. Sahid, Ansh, Adnan, Anant, Ayan, Jatin. Ansh Rastogi holds the kitty. ₹2,000 each. Zepto, Blinkit and the local kirana. Atta, Amul Taaza, dahi, eggs, onions, tomatoes, the gas cylinder, the water cans. No invented flatmate, no invented shop.

October 2026 adds up across every screen: ₹5,750 carried over + ₹8,000 of top-ups (4 of 6 in) − ₹2,350 spent = ₹11,400 in the kitty, 9 entries, ≈₹336 a day. Ansh owes back ₹340 to Sahid and ₹1,110 to Anant = ₹1,450.

Screen 1 · the most important one

Home

It does not exist today and its absence is the biggest complaint. Asana's skeleton (date line → greeting → one alert → list → recent) with Zopa's snapshot block dropped in. Two versions, because personalisation is the entire point.

“this doesn't have a proper home screen. It just opens in a card, and the card doesn't feel like it should be the home screen.”

The app no longer opens on the Kitty ledger, which is a document, not a home. It opens on a dashboard addressed to you by name.

“the prominent one should show who owes what — if the flat owes Anant, Anant should have it on his home screen”

The alert card is personal and there is exactly one. Sahid sees "₹340 to get back from Ansh" and can trace it in Recent two rows down. Ansh sees "₹1,450 to pay back to 2 mates" — his own outstanding work, which today is three screens deep behind the people icon (audit M2).

“Quick Add stays but shrinks”

One horizontally scrolling row of 48pt chips, not a grid. It costs one row of height instead of four, and Recent survives above the fold.

One brass element: the kitty block. Section links (Edit, See all, Record one) are underlined ink, not brass — brass on a link would be the second accent on the screen. Brass is spent exactly once per screen: the kitty block on Home, the selected day on Duties, the filled tile on Spending, the one pill on Manage / Import / an empty state. Kitchen and the + sheet spend it nowhere, which is allowed — one is a ceiling, not a quota. Position, duty points, duties and Recent are all white cards with ink and muted text. The alert sits on warm amber with its own word, never red — you are not in trouble.

Ansh's top-ups block obeys rule 3: initials with ✓/○, alphabetical, no amounts, no ranking, no "who's late" list. The only number is "4 of 6".

Check thisAnsh has two treasurer facts — ₹1,450 to pay back, and 4 of 6 topped up. Only the first is an alert. The second is a block further down. Is that the right call, or should the missing top-ups shout louder on the 1st?

Screen 4

Duties

ref-1's date strip lands here — not on Home, because Home is "today" and a date strip invites scrolling through a week of chores, which is this tab's job.

“only the assigned person ticks their own duty, and anyone else can vouch it was done”

Someone else's row gets a muted ring with no press feedback and their name beside it — never a tap that gets rejected. Once it is done, a Vouch pill appears next to it, which is the other half of the rule you decided.

Changed after the first draft · Skip is gone

There is no Skip button, no Skip swipe and no Skip anywhere. A skip and a no-show already produced identical output from the fairness engine — both simply sit outside the done points — so Skip was a word that bought nothing, while turning "formally decline a chore" into a one-tap, first-class action. In a six-person flat that is corrosive. What replaces it is Missed, which is computed, never recorded: past its due date with no Done. Nothing is appended to the ledger for a non-action, and no credit is earned, exactly as before. Skip rows already in the ledger stay valid; the app just stops writing new ones.

Changed after the first draft · Swap became Offer → Claim

With Skip gone, a one-sided swap would have become a "dump it on Ayan" button, which is worse than the thing we removed. So it is two-sided now. The assignee offers; the row stays open and everybody sees it; another mate taps Take it and it is theirs. Nobody is volunteered without tapping. And if nobody takes it by the due date it is missed like any other — the offer protects nobody, which is what stops Offer quietly becoming Skip again.

“swap and skip become swipe gestures, not buttons”

Still a swipe — but one panel, not two, because there is only one action left. Tapping the row open shows Offer as a button too; a swipe is never the only way in.

“I can't have Monday as my duty because of my college. I can do Tuesday”

Rota order lives on Manage, behind an explicit Edit, with a grab handle on the right and a remove on the left. It cannot be a long-press drag on the live list, because the swipe already owns that gesture.

“assign 2 or 3 people to one duty”

"Who's in" is a multi-select of the six initials. Two checked means they alternate, and each turn is still one row with one owner — so "only the assignee ticks it" stays true without a second rule.

Offers work on a future duty: Thursday's bins are offered on Wednesday, which is when you know you cannot do them — not on the morning itself.

Not to be confused with reordering the rota. "I can't do Monday because of college, I can do Tuesday" is a permanent change and lives in Manage's Edit mode, drawn below. An offer is one instance, once.

Check thisA missed row is grey with the word "Missed" and nothing else — no colour, no icon, no count beside anyone's name. If the flat actually wants to see "Jatin has missed 3 this month" somewhere, that is a different screen and a different argument. Say if you want it.

Screen 5

Spending

ref-2's vocabulary — 2-up stat tiles, big figures, the dot-matrix grid — with two of your references deliberately declined. The reasons are printed on the phone itself so you can overrule them.

“I liked this kind of stats and graphic”

Four tiles 2-up and exactly one filled. The fill has a reason — it is the one you picked, and it redraws the chart above — which also satisfies the one-accent rule for free.

Declined · ref-2's trend curve

It has no axis and no units; it is decoration laid over bars that already say the same thing. A bare bar chart with a right-hand scale and today bolded is more legible. If a trend line is wanted later it gets its own card, with real dots and a date axis.

Declined · ref-5's progress ring

A ring says "% of a goal". The kitty has no goal, it has a balance. The one place it would have earned its keep — top-ups, 4 of 6 — reads better as six initials on Ansh's home.

The dot grid is the highest-value chart here because it answers a real question at a glance: which days did this flat spend nothing? One hue at three steps, with a legend that spells each step out in words.

By store, not by category. The audit found the category chart is a single bar labelled "Other" in every month, forever, because nothing in the shipped app can set a category. Store is already recorded on every spend, so this version is true today.

Check this"Avg per day ₹336" is the tile; the audit (M4) wanted "≈₹350/day — about 12 days left" as a sentence under the kitty hero instead. One of the two should win. Which?

Import, merged

You are right that these were one action wearing two names. One entry, one reader, five stores.

“Import a Zepto order and photo of the bill are the same action”

Merged. Pick where it came from, hand over a screenshot or a photo, check the lines. The reader does not care which you gave it.

“wants import across Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket…”

The store picker covers Zepto, Blinkit, Instamart, BigBasket, JioMart and the kirana. This is genuinely an afternoon's work — IMPORT_OPTIONS §6 item 1: the extractor is not Zepto-specific, only one enum is, so widening it ships the feature. What is not possible is reading a cart or an order: none of these has a public API, and their terms forbid scraping it.

11pm. Ayan orders ₹1,180 on Zepto: ₹700 of flat groceries, ₹480 of his own chips and Coke.

The per-line Flat | Mine toggle (audit M1). "Mine" drops the line from the kitty and subtracts it from the total, so the saved figure still matches his bank statement. The whole raw bill is kept either way. The balance bar states the arithmetic out loud rather than just going green.

Check this"Mine" currently assumes the line belongs to whoever is importing. If Ayan imports a bill and the Coke was Jatin's, should the toggle open a six-name picker, or is that a rarity not worth the tap?

Screen 7

The + sheet

Pinterest's three-tile sheet, at four. A short sheet rising from the bottom with a grab handle, a close, and labelled rounded-square tiles.

“tap the centre +, a squarish rounded-corner menu comes up”

Exactly that. Four tiles: Log a spend · Import an order · Top up · Add to the list. Every tile is labelled — "Top up" and "Log a spend" are indistinguishable at 24pt as icons, and they always will be.

"Scan a receipt" is gone from the four, because it merged into Import. That freed the slot for "Add to the list", which is the 11pm action.

Declined · the radial / arc menu

No app in the Mobbin sweep ships one, and the reason is mechanical: the + sits at the bottom-centre, so an arc puts half its targets under your own knuckle and the other half out of reach without regripping. A sheet puts every target in the same band.

The tile is role-aware. For Sahid it is "Pay Ansh ₹2,000" (the existing upi:// link). For Ansh it is "Record a top-up". Same slot, two different jobs — this is drawn on the two homes above.

Both versions of the sheet are drawn in the nav question above, since the difference between the two nav options is what the sheet contains.

Screen 8

Empty states

Buddy's template, which every good empty state in the sweep shares: one drawn object → headline → one line of body → one action, never two. Two of the five are drawn here.

One line-drawn object in one colour — an empty bowl, a flat wallet. Flat-vector illustrated people would cost a designer and would fight the canvas; a stroked SVG works in both modes and leaves the accent free for the button.

The second one has no button on purpose. "Nobody's out of pocket" is good news, not a task. An empty state with a button on it implies you have failed to do something.

The other three, already written: Kitty in a new month ("Nothing out of the kitty yet" → Log a spend), Duties all done ("Every duty's done today. Next one is Adnan's, tomorrow." → no button), and a month with no entries ("No entries in March" → View another month as a text link, not a button).

Honest list

Still open — what this concept does not answer

Everything below was flagged by the patterns sweep, the audit or the brief, and is not settled by these screens.

Closed since the first draft: the "Escape option should also show Suraj for that day" question. Skip has been removed from the app, so there is nothing left for it to show.

  • The paletteNot chosen. These screens are drawn in a placeholder derived from ref-4. Dark mode is not drawn at all.
  • Bills — rent, electricity, Wi-Fi, the maidStill split on WhatsApp, still out of scope. Two in-rules shapes exist (pay them from the kitty and raise the ₹2,000, or a second pot with its own holder) and this is a scope decision, not a design one. Not on any screen here.
  • GuestsAdnan's cousin stays four nights and eats every meal; the flat eats six-and-a-bit people's groceries and Adnan pays a sixth. Nothing here adds a guest weight. It changes the fairness model, so it is your call.
  • Moving outNothing in the app can write a leaving date or show a final settle. Jatin's lease ending is a real event with no screen. Not drawn here.
  • The product metadata forkCategory, minimum quantity, base unit and pack size have no writer anywhere in the shipped app. That is why Spending here shows stores instead of categories and why the fridge shows "7 left" rather than "1 L". Either a product editor plus a seed of the real basket gets built, or the features that depend on it get cut. These screens assume cut, which may be the wrong assumption.
  • The receipt photoThe bill image used for extraction is thrown away once the LLM has read it. "₹1,180? what was that?" has no answer. Import here does not show a kept photo.
  • NotificationsThere is a Notifications row on Profile and no push behind it. Nothing in the app notifies anyone of anything today.
  • Deep links into Zepto and BlinkitDrawn on Kitchen as "Open in Blinkit". Whether the app lands on the search results or just its own home screen is unverified — it needs one tap on a real phone with the app installed before it is promised.
  • CompareifyIt is Comparify, it is real, and it takes no payments — it hands you to Zepto. There is no card to add and no catalogue to buy. Nothing here uses it.
  • Motionref-6's card-expanding-into-a-sheet is the motion language for all of this, and a still page cannot show it. The three places it goes (a pool entry, a duty row, a month card) are decided; the animation is not drawn.
