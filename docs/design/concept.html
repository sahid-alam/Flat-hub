V3 — the concept screens, round two

Nineteen screens at true phone size (390×844), redrawn after your thirteen notes. This page is about what is on each screen and where it sits. Every screen carries a note saying which complaint it answers, in your own words, and the eight things that changed since the last version are marked ROUND 2 in brass down the left.

Dark mode is here. Use the Light / Dark button at the left of the index bar. The page opens in whichever your phone is set to. Every screen is drawn once and flips with the switch, rather than being drawn twice — it makes the page half as long to scroll and shows you more, not less.

The colours are still a placeholder. Nobody has chosen a palette. These warm tones are from ref-4 so the screens can be drawn at all. Judge what appears on a screen, in what order, and what you tap to reach it.

The dashed line on a tall screen is the fold — 844pt, where an iPhone stops. Anything under it needs a scroll. The floating bar is drawn at the bottom of each drawing so it does not sit on the content you are meant to read; on a real phone it stays pinned at the fold and content passes behind it.

The house rules still hold: one accent per screen, every coloured state also carries a word, "Pay"/"Get back" and never "owe", no per-mate leaderboard, Indian digit grouping, tabular figures. The contrast table below is computed from the hex values in both modes, not claimed.

The data is this flat's. Sahid, Ansh, Adnan, Anant, Ayan, Jatin. Ansh Rastogi holds the kitty. ₹2,000 each. Blinkit, Zepto and the local kirana. No invented flatmate, no invented shop. Everything is drawn as Wednesday 7 October so the figures hold together: ₹5,750 carried over + ₹8,000 of top-ups (4 of 6 in) − ₹2,350 spent = ₹11,400 in the kitty, 9 entries, ≈₹336 a day. Ansh owes back ₹340 to Sahid and ₹1,110 to Anant.

Screen 4 · a new feature

Meals, and the cook

Whoever is on dishes tells the cook what to make and gets what is missing onto the list. You asked for a reminder, and for it to show on the home screen.

Round 2 · Modelled as a role, not a chore name

A duty carries a "plans the meals" switch. Today's planner is whoever is assigned that duty — the app never looks for the word "dishes". Rename the chore, or move meal-planning to cooking, and nothing breaks.

With a two-person duty, both of you can write it, and the last one to write wins. This is a note to the cook, not a ledger, so overwriting is fine — unlike money, which is append-only forever.

The groceries half: the plan is checked against the fridge and the staples, and anything it cannot find is offered as one button onto the shopping list. That is the only automatic path onto the list; everything else is typed by a person.

Where the reminder lives: on the planner's own home, as the card with two empty fields. It is not a push notification — notifications are being worked out separately.

Screen 5

Duties

ref-1's date strip lands here. Skip is gone, Swap is now Offer → Claim, and co-assignment has been redrawn because we had it wrong.

Round 2 · Two people means together, not alternating

You wrote: "what I meant by two people is that one duty will be fulfilled by two people. e.g., Adnan and me one day, Ayan and Ansh the other day." We drew them taking turns. Wrong, and it is fixed: one duty, one block, two rows on the same day. Adnan has ticked his, yours is open. Each of you earns the points separately, each of you can be missed separately, and your partner is the obvious voucher.

Round 2 · Photos on a duty

You asked for "an option to add photos if someone didnt do his duty correctly." It attaches to the turn, not to the person, and anyone can add one. The row says how many and who added it — no "report", no flag, no red. Same discipline as the Missed row: a record, not an accusation.

“only the assigned person ticks their own duty, and anyone else can vouch it was done”

Someone else's row gets a muted ring with no press feedback and their name beside it — never a tap that gets rejected. Once done, a Vouch pill appears.

Skip is gone

A skip and a no-show already produced identical output from the fairness engine, so Skip was a word that bought nothing while making "formally decline a chore" a one-tap action. Missed replaces it and is computed, never recorded. Old skip rows in the ledger stay valid.

Swap became Offer → Claim

Two-sided, so nobody is volunteered. If nobody takes it by the due date it is missed like any other — the offer protects nobody. On a pair duty you offer your half.

Screen 6 · reworked, not restyled

Manage duties

You said plainly that you did not like the structure of the add-duty screen. It is a different screen now, in two parts.

Round 2 · The list comes first

You wrote: "I honestly didn't like the structure and ui of the add duties things, we can have rework on it." Manage now opens on the four duties this flat actually runs; tapping one edits it; Add a duty is a separate button at the top. Before, it opened on an empty form — the rarest thing you do here.

The form is grouped, not stacked. Three cards instead of one column of chip rows: when and what it is worth, who does it, what else it carries. The same fields, in a shape you can skim.

It shows you the rota it is about to make. Pick "2 at once" and six names, and the form prints Wed Adnan + Sahid / Thu Ansh + Ayan / Fri Anant + Jatin before you save. That is the actual rework — a form that tells you the consequence instead of making you imagine it.

The rota is pairs now, three rows not six, with the grab handle on each pair. "I can't have Monday because of college" means dragging your pair down one row, and it is still a permanent change — distinct from an Offer, which is one instance, once.

Retire, never delete. Deleting a duty would rewrite history the points were earned from, so an old duty goes to a Retired list and its rows stay in the ledger.

You asked for the records. spend.receipt_path has existed in the schema since day one and nothing has ever written to it — the photo the reader looks at is thrown away. Now it is kept and shows on the statement row, so "₹1,180? what was that?" has an answer.

11pm. Ayan orders ₹1,180: ₹700 of flat groceries, ₹480 of his own chips and Coke.

Screen 9 · three missing features

Moving out, away and guests

All three change what somebody pays, and none of them exists in the app today.

Round 2 · Moving out

Nothing in the app can write a leaving date, and there is no final-settle screen, so "Jatin's lease ends on the 20th and he wants his money" happens on WhatsApp. Pick the date, see the arithmetic, freeze it. The wording follows the house rule — "Jatin gets back ₹460", never "owes".

Round 2 · Guests

The mirror of Away, which is why they are drawn side by side. A guest-night adds one person-day to the host's share and nobody else's — Adnan's cousin stays four nights, Adnan pays about ₹210 more, everyone else is untouched. Same month-close guard as an absence: you cannot log a guest into a closed month.

Away has never been drawn before, even though it is built. It is here so the symmetry is visible: one pauses your share, the other adds to it, and they are the same form.

Screen 10

The + sheet

Pinterest's three-tile sheet, at four. A short sheet rising from the bottom with a grab handle, a close, and labelled rounded-square tiles.

Round 2 · It grows out of the + button

You wrote: "the animation should look like coming from the + icon/button." The sheet's transform-origin is the + circle itself — bottom-right, where your thumb just was — so it scales up from that point rather than sliding in from the bottom edge. Reversed on close. 240 ms, no bounce; under reduced motion it cross-fades in place. The dashed circle on Option A marks the origin.

“tap the centre +, a squarish rounded-corner menu comes up”

Exactly that. Every tile is labelled — "Top up" and "Log a spend" are indistinguishable at 24pt as icons, and always will be.

Declined · the radial / arc menu

No app in the sweep ships one. The + sits at the bottom-centre, so an arc puts half its targets under your own knuckle and the other half out of reach without regripping.

The tile is role-aware. For Sahid "Pay Ansh ₹2,000" (the upi:// link); for Ansh "Record a top-up".

Both versions of the sheet are drawn in the nav question above, since the difference between the two nav options is what the sheet contains.

Still open — what this concept does not answer

Not settled by these screens. The first three are new questions that round two created.

Closed since the last version: the Suraj question (Skip is gone, so there is nothing for it to show); guests, moving out and the kept receipt, all now drawn.

  • Does the kitty tile carry enough weight? Asked on screen 1. The hero shrank because you asked it to, but nav Option A depends on Home always being the door to the ledger. A brass tile is a door. Your call, and it is the one thing on this page I would not ship without hearing back on.
  • Where does the morning summary come from? Two honest options. Templated from facts the app already has — free, instant, offline, and it will read the same by the third week. An LLM call each morning — warmer and more varied, about ₹1 a day for six people, needs a network, and can be wrong in a way a template cannot. Either way the rules are the same: never name another mate's miss, never rank anyone, never say "owe", never repeat the alert card. Which?
  • Is "Kitchen" gone for good? Making the fridge its own section means the word Kitchen no longer labels anything — the fridge, the shopping list and the meals are three separate places. That is cleaner, but if you think of them as one thing, say so and they get a hub screen instead.
  • Barcode dataThe scan button on Add a product is drawn, and whether a lookup exists for the packs this flat buys is still being tested. If the hit rate is poor the button goes and the typing stays. Nothing else on the page depends on it.
  • Where duty photos liveDrawn storage-agnostic. Size limits, who can delete one, and how long they are kept are all undecided — and "who can delete" is the one that matters, because a photo of a dirty floor is evidence until the person it is about removes it.
  • NotificationsYou asked for proper notifications for everything. Being worked out separately; the Profile row is a door to a screen that is not drawn.
  • Bills — rent, electricity, Wi-Fi, the maidStill split on WhatsApp, still out of scope. Two in-rules shapes exist (pay them from the kitty and raise the ₹2,000, or a second pot with its own holder) and it is a scope decision, not a design one.
  • The product metadata forkCategory, minimum quantity, base unit and pack size have no writer anywhere in the shipped app. That is why Spending shows stores instead of categories and the fridge shows "7 left" rather than "1 L". The barcode scan would fix a lot of this at once, which is another reason the data test matters.
  • Deep links into Zepto and BlinkitDrawn on the shopping list as "Open in Blinkit". Whether the app lands on the search results or just its own home screen is unverified and needs one tap on a real phone.
  • CompareifyIt is Comparify, it is real, and it takes no payments — it hands you to Zepto. There is no card to add and no catalogue to buy.
  • MotionThe + sheet's origin is specified above. ref-6's card-expanding-into-a-sheet is the language for the rest, and a still page cannot show it — the three places it goes (a pool entry, a duty row, a month card) are decided; the animation is not drawn.
